Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 | Website & App: Mpoukitses (mpoukitses.com)

Mpoukitses ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mpoukitses website (mpoukitses.com) and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our recipe and meal-planning service:

  • Local App Preferences: Shopping lists, favorite recipes, and ingredient filters are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly create an account.
  • Account & Subscription Information (Optional): If you subscribe to Pro features or contact support, we may collect your email address and subscription status via Apple App Store, Google Play, or our payment processor.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Basic, anonymized diagnostic and performance logs (such as device type and crash reports) to keep the app fast and reliable.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display recipes, filter ingredients, and maintain your grocery list.
  • To manage optional Pro subscriptions and restore purchases across devices.
  • To respond to customer support requests and improve app stability.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Where applicable, trusted infrastructure providers (such as Cloudflare for web hosting and Apple/Google for in-app subscriptions) process technical data strictly in accordance with their privacy standards and GDPR requirements.

4. Children's Privacy

Mpoukitses is designed for parents and caregivers preparing meals for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 16.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You can clear locally stored shopping lists and preferences at any time by clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app. If you have created an account or shared your email with us, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us directly.
